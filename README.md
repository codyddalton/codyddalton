Hey, it's me! Check this out. I've been working with a great non-government organization called ICAAD (International Center for Advocates Against Discrimination). In-between trips to the U.N, Jaspreet Singh reached out to me for my help with a new Artivism project (art activism). He needed a volunteer to help translate a real-life exhibit into an online experience. The exhibit in question was the work of artist Dilpreet Bhullar, who put together a thought-provoking gallery relating to the hardships and struggles of migrating refugees. 

With Dilpreet and Jaspreet's guidance, I put together an interactive web exhibit which I hope only adds to the poignancy of its source. The exhibit is now live and can be found at: https://constantflux.icaad.ngo/
